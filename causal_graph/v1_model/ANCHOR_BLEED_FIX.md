## 1. What was actually wrong: the detector keyed on the wrong variable

`SCALE_CALIBRATION_CHECK.md`'s Finding 3 specified that off-target **confidence ≥ High**
is the signal that a vignette leaked across dimensions. `run_vignette_probe.py`
implemented that faithfully (`elif d.confidence >= 0.6`). The specified variable is wrong,
so the error propagated spec → instrument → recorded finding, and each stage looked
correct against the one before it.

The right variable is **displacement off the neutral 0.5 band**. A dimension the vignette
says nothing about should read 0.5. Confidence describes how sure the scorer is of
whatever it landed on — a different question from whether it landed somewhere it
shouldn't have.

Recomputed from the raw CSVs of rounds 1–3 (630 rows), nothing re-run:

| relationship | what the raw rows show | old detector | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| collaboration vignette → autonomy | **0.5 (neutral) on 20/20 runs**; confidence 0.6/0.3/0.1 across the three round-1 vignettes | fired | **not a bleed — withdrawn** |
| autonomy vignette → collaboration | **0.3 on 28/30 runs** at autonomy ≥ 0.5, confidence 0.1–0.3 | silent | **real — promoted** |
| autonomy vignette → innovation | **19/20 runs**, tracking level: autonomy 0.1 → innovation 0.1, autonomy 0.9 → innovation 0.7 | silent | **real — promoted** |

§5 of the calibration doc recorded the first row as a confirmed 10/10 finding and
dismissed the third as "1/5, incidental." Both are corrected in place in that doc.

A second gap compounded it: the probe logged quote *counts* and discarded quote *text* —
the one datum that separates a vignette artifact from an anchor defect. 630 rows were
scored without it, and isolating the cause now needs a re-run purely to recover text the
API already returned each time.

## 2. Why the two surviving bleeds are plausible in the anchors

Read against the frozen anchor text, both have a concrete mechanism:

**Collaboration's 0.1 band contradicts Collaboration's own `keep_distinct`.**

> `keep_distinct`: "Collective teams can still hand individuals full ownership of the how.
> Score meeting and team density here, decision rights under Autonomy."
>
> band 0.1: "**Individual ownership**, heads-down norm, low meeting load…"

The band opens with the exact construct the guard just assigned to Autonomy. Band text is
what the scorer matches against, so the band wins and the guard is dead letter.
`where_to_look` compounds it by telling raters to look for **"own your area"** language
under Collaboration — verbatim the construct Autonomy's 0.7 band names ("*Ownership*,
results-over-process language present").

**Innovation's low pole is an ordinary English verb, and it has no Autonomy guard.**
The pole is named **"Execute / optimize."** The round-1 autonomy-low vignette says a brief
"spells out exactly how to **execute** it" — a pure statement about decision rights that
lexically matches Innovation's low pole by accident. Innovation's `keep_distinct` guards
against Pace only; the measured coupling is with Autonomy, which is unguarded.

## 3. What is fixed now

**`run_vignette_probe.py`** — detector rewritten to trigger on displacement off neutral
(≥ 0.2, one full band), not confidence. Adds an `evidence_quotes` column and a
`displacement` column, one flag per off-target dimension instead of one per run, and a
cross-dimension rollup that aggregates across levels — the resolution at which the finding
actually lives, since autonomy → collaboration is invisible at any single level and
unmistakable across five. New `--anchors` / `--allow-rubric-mismatch` flags allow an
alternate rubric to be A/B-tested **without editing the frozen file**.

Verified offline by replaying round 3's real CSV through the patched logic with
`score_packet` stubbed — zero API calls. The patched detector surfaces all three findings
above from data already collected, and labels autonomy → innovation
`[VARIES WITH LEVEL — suspect a coupling, not a constant offset]`.

**`anchors_v2_PROPOSED.json`** — generated by `make_anchors_v2.py` as a minimal diff
asserted against every v1 string it replaces, so it fails loudly rather than drifting if
`anchors.json` moves. Nine leaf fields change and no others: Collaboration bands 0.1/0.3
and `where_to_look` rewritten in interaction-density terms only; Innovation's low pole
renamed *Refine / optimize*; Autonomy guard added to Innovation's `keep_distinct` and
Innovation guard added to Autonomy's; `_note` and `rubric_version`.

**`vignette_probe_pairs_v4_bleed_diagnostic.yaml`** — the v3 autonomy vignettes with
incidental off-dimension vocabulary removed and nothing else changed. Person-side item
choices are copied verbatim from v3 and verified identical.
