# Trying Solverah — a guided tour

Solverah is a career-development platform. It measures how you work, compares that
with what your current job actually asks of you, and tells you what to do about the
gap. It also covers how to work with the manager you have and which career paths fit
your skills. Everything below is live on **solverah.com** today, and you can try every
step yourself.

**Time.** About 45 minutes for the full tour. If you have 15, do the short tour.

**Use a laptop or desktop.** The product works on a phone, but a few screens (the
FocusType scenes, the landing page's diagram) are at their best on a larger screen.

---

## What you'll notice first

- **No scores.** Solverah never shows you a number for "how good a fit" you are. Work
  Pulse, the core assessment, ends without a score on purpose. A score invites people
  to answer the way that sounds most employable, and then the measurement stops
  describing them. Reports use plain words ("Straining", "Notable", "Aligned") and
  concrete next steps instead.
- **Private by design.** Employers never see your answers. Your manager is never shown
  your manager profile. You can download all your data, or delete your account,
  from your account settings at any time.
- **Forced choices.** Most questions ask you to pick between two real, reasonable ways
  of working. Neither answer is the "right" one.

## The full tour

### 1 · First look, no account needed (3 minutes)

1. Open **solverah.com** and scroll slowly. The top of the page is a short story in
   five steps. The example result cards change as you go. They are labelled as
   examples; yours come from your own answers.
2. Keep scrolling to **"Six instruments, one profile."** Hover over each row of the
   list to see how each instrument feeds the same profile.
3. Click **"Preview a quiz"**, choose **Early Career**, then **"Start quiz"**.
4. Answer the ten questions honestly and click **"Get Insight"**. An AI-written insight
   comes back in a few seconds.
5. Click **"View insights"** to read it in full: a summary, key takeaways and next
   steps.

**Why it matters:** you get something useful before you give us an email address.

### 2 · Create your account (2 minutes)

1. From the insights page, click **"Create account"**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 12 characters with an upper-case
   letter, a lower-case letter, a number and a symbol.
3. We email you a **6-digit code** from `no-reply@solverah.com`. It expires after 15
   minutes. If it isn't in your inbox within a minute, check spam.
4. Enter the code, then sign in. You land back on your quiz insights, already saved
   to your account.

**Why it matters:** nothing you did as a guest is lost when you sign up.

### 3 · Tell us where you are in your career (1 minute)

Do this before the Situation Report, because the report needs to know whether you
have a job right now.

1. Click **Profile** in the header, then the **Experience** tab.
2. Under **"Where are you in your career?"**, choose **"Currently employed"**. It saves
   straight away.

### 4 · Work Pulse — how you work (8 minutes)

1. From the dashboard, click **"Start now"** on the Work Pulse banner.
2. Answer 24 either-or questions about the conditions you do your best work in.
3. When you finish, you see **"That's everything we need"**, and no score.

**What to look for:** the ending is deliberate. Work Pulse feeds everything else on
the platform, and keeping its numbers hidden keeps the answers honest. The **"How
this works, and what we hold back →"** link on the intro page explains why.

**Tip:** if you leave part-way, Solverah offers to pick up where you left off when you
come back in the same browser tab.

### 5 · The Situation Report — you, and your job (10 minutes)

This is the centre of the product.

1. On the Work Pulse completion screen, click **"Describe your job →"**.
2. Answer 16 questions about the job you have now: how much say you have, the pace,
   how decisions get made. Describe the job as it actually is, not as it was
   advertised. About five minutes.
3. The report builds straight away.

**What you'll see:** **"You, and this job"**, six areas of work ranked by how far apart
you and the job sit. Each area reads Straining, Notable or Aligned, with a direction
("Less than you need" or "More than you need"). Open any area for three kinds of help:

- **"Try a change"** — a small two-week experiment you can run in the job you already
  have, with steps and a way to tell whether it worked.
- **"Negotiate it"** — who to ask, how to open the conversation, and what to say.
- **"If it won't move"** — why some conditions rarely change, and what staying costs.

Click **"Open all"**, then **"Print this report"**, for a complete document.

**Why it matters:** the report doesn't tell you to quit or to stay. It shows you where
the friction is and gives you something concrete to try first.

**Tip:** answer about a real job with some real friction. A job that suits you on
every dimension produces a short report.

### 6 · FocusType — how you concentrate (5 minutes)

1. Click **Intelligence** in the header and press **"Start FocusType™ →"**.
2. Click **"Begin"** and work through five short interactive scenes: dragging,
   sorting and timed choices. Each scene is final once you finish it.
3. You get a named focus style, a one-line description, workplace recommendations
   and the six factors behind your result.

### 7 · The Manager Decoder — the manager you have (10 minutes)

Some of what feels like "the job" is really one person. The Decoder separates the two.

1. On the **Intelligence** page, press **"Start ManagerMatch™ →"**. Fifteen choices tell us what you
   need from a manager. Results take under a minute.
2. Then **"Now profile your actual manager →"**: 16 questions about how your manager
   actually works, not how you feel about it.
3. You get **"Your managing-up playbook"**: where to focus first, then for each area the
   reason, the request to make, what to bring to your next one-to-one, and the words
   to use.

**Why it matters:** the playbook is private to you, and it turns a vague "my manager
and I don't click" into a specific conversation you can have this week.

### 8 · Path Intelligence — where your skills could take you (3 minutes)

1. On the **Intelligence** page, click **"View Your Paths →"**.
2. The first time, Solverah asks you to add a few skills, rate them, and answer three
   quick questions about what you want next.
3. Career paths appear in a few seconds, drawn from the U.S. Department of Labor's
   O\*NET occupation data. A plain-language reason why each one fits fades in
   shortly after.
4. Click a path to see the training it usually takes compared with yours, the skills
   you already have, what you would need to pick up, and the actual daily tasks.
5. Tap **"Not for me"** on one path and watch the list adjust.

**Why it matters:** no percentages. Every line is a plain statement about the work.

### 9 · Pulse — a weekly check-in (1 minute)

1. From the dashboard's Pulse card, take your first check-in: three questions plus
   one on how work has been going overall.
2. The value builds over weeks. Each check-in adds a point to your trend, and you can
   log changes like "New manager" or "Reorg" so you can see their effect.
3. On the trend page you choose how often we ask: weekly, every two weeks, monthly or
   off. If you stop answering, Solverah slows down rather than nagging.

### 10 · Your data, your control (1 minute)

1. Click the **gear icon** in the header to open **Account settings**.
2. Click **"Download a copy of your data"** and open the file: your profile,
   your answers and your check-ins, as a JSON file.
3. **"Delete my account"** is there too, behind a typed confirmation. If you try it,
   use an account you don't need, because it is permanent.
4. For the reasoning behind all of this, read **"How this works, and what we hold
   back"** (linked from the Intelligence page).

### 11 · The AI assistant (optional, 2 minutes)

Click **Agent** in the header and ask a career question about your own background. A
good one to try: "Given my background, what should I look for in my next manager?" It
uses your profile and resume. Like any AI, it can make mistakes.

## What's coming next

These are built or in progress, and are marked "coming soon" where they appear today:

- **CompanyLens.** Name a company, and Solverah researches its working culture from
  public evidence. You see how it reads on each area of work, with dated, sourced
  evidence. Where the evidence is mixed, both sides are shown rather than averaged.
- **Tolerance and must-haves.** No job fits perfectly. You'll be able to say how much
  give you have on each area and name up to two non-negotiables. Reports and company
  reads then respect them.
- **Feed and search.** The header's **Feed** item says "coming soon" for now.
- **Work Pulse Pro**, a paid tier, is on the roadmap.

---

## If something looks off

- **There's no score after Work Pulse.** That is intended (see "What you'll notice
  first").
- **The verification code didn't arrive.** Check spam. Codes expire after 15 minutes;
  use **"Resend code"** once.
- **Feed or a "coming soon" label.** Those parts are still being built.
- **Anything else.** Write down the page you were on and what you expected, and send
  it to us. That feedback is exactly what this tour is for.
