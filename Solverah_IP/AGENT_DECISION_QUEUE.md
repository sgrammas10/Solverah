### [2026-08-19b] S-18's primary statistic: keep the composition test, or switch to the climb?

**A decision, not a correction — and it must be made before any respondent exists.**

`STATE.md` named the primary as **conditional composition**: among respondents scoring 0.5 on a
dimension, the share who arrived via `{1,1,0,0}`, feedback vs the others. Simulating it before
writing a threshold around it turned up a ceiling: `{1,1,0,0}` is the e=0 pattern, so under the
NULL it already holds **0.76** of that stratum in every dimension. Rule-6 respondents raise a
share that is nearly maxed out, so a 20% rule-6 prevalence moves it only +0.10.

**The alternative measures the climb across the variable change** —
`q_d = P(rung3 | rung1, rung2)`, feedback vs pooled others. In every other dimension rungs 2→3
is one variable getting harder; in feedback alone it crosses delay → audience, so the
audience-averse stop there however far up the delay ladder they climbed. Null q = 0.46,
mid-range. Base is ~57% of respondent-dimensions vs ~35% for the 0.5 stratum. Effect ~1.5x.

**Power at phi=0.15** (150 trials/cell, ~±0.04): N=100 → 0.26 composition vs **0.37** climb;
N=300 → 0.55 vs **0.82**; N=500 → 0.79 vs **0.96**. Both calibrated (FPR 0.01–0.05 vs nominal
0.05); the climb statistic is mildly conservative.

**Proposed: climb becomes primary, composition is retained as a pre-registered secondary.**

**The cost of declining:** none to validity. Keeping composition as primary raises the N
threshold from **300 to ~500** first-sittings.

Default if unanswered: the pre-registration stays PROPOSED and neither analysis runs.
