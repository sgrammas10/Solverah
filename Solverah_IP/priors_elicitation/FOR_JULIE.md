# Covering note draft, for Sebastian to send or rewrite

---



Attached is a short form, about fifteen minutes, that you can do whenever you have a
gap. It saves as you go, so you can close it halfway through and come back and if you
only get through part of it, send it anyway and tell me where you stopped. 

**What it's for.** The matching model needs a starting hypothesis: how much does a
mismatch on each of the six dimensions actually hurt someone's chances in a role. Right
now the code assumes all six matter exactly equally, and that wanting *more* autonomy
than a job offers is precisely as bad as wanting *less*. Nobody thinks that's true it's
a placeholder sitting there until you weigh in, and it's currently the single biggest
piece of guesswork in the whole thing.

**What it asks.** Twelve judgement calls, all the same shape: I describe a person and a
role that don't line up on one dimension, and you say how many out of ten people like
that are still doing well a year later. Nothing technical. I do the conversion.

**Two things worth saying up front.** Neither end of any dimension is the good end you're
only judging the mismatch, not whether a company is nice. And there's an explicit "I'd
rather not put a number on this one" for every dimension. Use it where you mean it; the
model treats it as *lean on the data instead of on Julie here*, which is a real answer and
more useful than a tidy-looking number you don't believe.

This is separate from the anchors rewrite that one still needs your read when you have
a longer stretch. This one you can do in a waiting room.
