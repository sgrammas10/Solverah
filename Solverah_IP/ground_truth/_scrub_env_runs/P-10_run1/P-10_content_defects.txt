CONTENT DEFECTS — P-10
  Found during the de-identification pass, which is the second close read this packet gets
  before a rater sees it. NOTHING BELOW WAS CORRECTED. Each is reproduced as found and
  scrubbed around, per the scrub brief: a defect fixed here is a defect that disappears
  from the build record, and the build is what needs correcting.

CD-1. SECTION 4 (ANONYMIZATION PRE-FLAGS) — the company-name occurrence list is wrong in three
      ways, and it is the input the scrub pass is told to read.
      · It attributes the derived self-referential employee term to section A excerpt 2. That
        excerpt contains neither the company name nor the derived term. The term is in section A
        excerpt 1, which the same sentence separately lists — so the entry is duplicated onto
        the wrong excerpt rather than merely missing.
      · It omits two section B reviews whose quote bodies do carry the company name: the reviews
        dated 2024-10-03 and 2024-01-05. Eight section B quote bodies carry the name or the
        derived term; the block names six.
      · It does not flag the one review HEADLINE containing the company name (the review dated
        2024-02-16), even though headlines are printed in the rater-facing attribution and are
        kept verbatim under A7.
      Severity: highest in the packet. The pre-flag block exists to tell the scrubber where the
      identifiers are, and a scrubber working from it instead of from a full-text sweep would
      have shipped the company name into a rater-facing packet three times. This pass swept the
      text mechanically and did not rely on the block; the block was used only to confirm the
      sweep, and it is the block that failed the comparison.

CD-2. SECTION 2b (EVIDENCE COVERAGE) cites evidence that is not in the packet. The FEEDBACK
      dimension is justified as "one values-page principle on debate norms, one job-posting line
      on advising senior leaders". No section C excerpt is about advising senior leaders; no
      section C line mentions seniority, leaders or advising at all. The nearest line concerns
      influencing compliance programmes and strategic direction, which is a different claim.
      This is the P-06 shape — a coverage judgement resting on a quote that is not where it is
      said to be — and it matters because FEEDBACK is the one dimension the packet marks THIN,
      so the two pieces of evidence named are load-bearing on that call.

CD-3. SECTION 2g (SCOPE BLOCK) accounts for 14 of the 15 reviews. It states "8 of 15 state
      'Remote' as their location" and then names San Francisco (3), Chicago, Atlanta and one
      probably mis-geocoded town: 8 + 3 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 14. The fifteenth review's location
      renders in lower case as "remote" and appears in neither tally. The remote share is
      therefore understated — 9 of 15 describe remote work, not 8 — in the same block a rater
      would use to judge dispersion.

CD-4. SECTION C carries a date that cannot be right, and the builder disclosed it in 2e/C7.
      Posting 3's detail-page "Date posted" field reads 2026-08-27, the same day the listing
      page described the role as having been posted two days earlier. Recorded here for
      completeness because the contradiction survives into the rater-facing packet: the packet
      prints the detail-page date with nothing to show that the same board gave a different one.
      Reproduced verbatim; not corrected.

CD-5. SECTION B contains a cross-reference into a section raters never see. One review's
      location field read "(see fidelity note — flagged, retained)", pointing at section 2e,
      which is build record and is removed by design. The scrub had to edit that field anyway
      under R5, because it carried a location; the pointer was therefore replaced with the
      substance it pointed at, so the rater keeps the warning rather than a dead reference. The
      edit is declared on the excerpt line and recorded in the diff. Recorded here as a
      BUILD-side defect: the collector should not put a QA-section cross-reference inside
      rater-facing text, and the durable fix belongs in the collector.
