Judgment calls made, and why:
  1. E1's "six major therapeutic areas" and D3's "3.5 months of review" / "six years of
     experience" were left untouched. None is a headcount, dollar figure, or percentage — the
     three categories R6/A1 names — and each carries dimension signal (breadth, pace) rather
     than functioning as a re-identifying metric on its own. Flagging rather than extending R6
     to a category it does not name.
  2. "New York" (the state) is kept literal throughout, including in the SCOPE DECLARATION and
     in E2. The SCOPE DECLARATION already tells the rater the review population is New York
     State-based; state-level geography is dispersion/population context, not a site-level
     identifier, and R5's worked examples all operate at city/site granularity. City-level and
     street-level strings (East Greenbush, Rensselaer, Sleepy Hollow, Tarrytown, the two minted
     sites) are the ones tokenised.
  3. The J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference name (E1) and the ATS/industry vocabulary in C
     (SOPs, GxP) are kept — third-party/industry-standard terms, not company fingerprints, per
     the industry-not-scrubbed finding and the precedent for keeping recognisable third-party
     platform/vendor names.
  4. C11's "Safety Sciences Strategy" was scrubbed under R4/A5 even though Section 4's pre-flag
     block never names it. This is an application of an already-decided rule to a term the block
     missed, not an extension of the rule to a new case — recorded as a pre-flag miss in the
