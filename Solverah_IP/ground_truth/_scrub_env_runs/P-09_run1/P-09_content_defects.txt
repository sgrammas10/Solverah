Found during the de-identification pass of 2026-09-07, reading the raw packet as the second

CD-1  Section 1 is not self-contained: four cross-references point into the build record.
      SEVERITY: real, low harm. Two are explicit pointers, two are unsupported claims.

      Section 1 is the only part a rater sees, and sections 2–5 are marked REMOVE BEFORE
      PACKET / NEVER IN THE PACKET, so anything Section 1 cites from them dangles in the
      rater-facing artifact. Verbatim, as built:

        A, excerpt 8, retrieval note:
          "needed JS: Y (renderer; this page was reached only through the site's own
          navigation, see fidelity note)"

        D, excerpt 2, date note:
          "report covers calendar year 2025; published 2026 (see fidelity note)"

      The fidelity note is section 2e. A rater told to "see" it has nothing to see, and in
      both cases the pointer is doing real work — the second one is the packet's only
      signal that the report's publication date is inferred rather than printed.

      Two section headers make the same shape of claim without a pointer:

        "--- D. FIRST-PERSON ACCOUNT (yield: 4 · ladder fully attempted) ---"
        "--- E. RECENT NEWS (yield: 3 · searches logged: 5) ---"

      "ladder fully attempted" and "searches logged: 5" are both assertions whose evidence
      is the section 2c ladder checklist. They read to a rater as a warranty about coverage
      that the packet does not carry.

      This is a build-prompt gap, not a builder error: nothing tells the builder that
      Section 1 must stand alone once the QA sections are removed. Either the caveats
      belong inline in Section 1, or the scrub pass needs a rule for carrying them across.
      No rule exists, so nothing was carried across and nothing was cut.

CD-2  Section E, excerpt 3, is company-authored material inside a section of press
      reporting, and it is counted in the section's yield.
      SEVERITY: observation. Correctly disclosed on the line; recorded for completeness.

      The section is titled RECENT NEWS and its other two excerpts are trade press. The
      third is an SEC-filed earnings release, tagged by the builder as

        "[company-authored earnings release filed with the SEC — NOT independent
        reporting]"

      so a rater is told plainly and cannot mistake it for reporting. The residue is that
      the header's "yield: 3" counts three items where only two are independent, which

      use the proposed 0.7 rule would put it to. The build already self-reports this as
      defect C-2, but C-2 lives in section 5, which no rater and no scorer reads. Recorded
      here so it survives into the record that follows the packet.
