The employee reviews in this packet come from truck drivers and driver-adjacent shop and
terminal staff — company drivers, recent CDL trainees, intermodal and tanker drivers, diesel
and trailer technicians, a driver leader and one customer service representative. Other parts
of this company — the corporate office, sales, information technology, engineering, finance,
legal and marketing organisations — are almost entirely absent from the review evidence, even

organisation.
