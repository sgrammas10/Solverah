CD-1  STATED COUNT DISAGREES WITH THE NUMBER OF ITEMS — section E header
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The section E marker reads:

    --- E. RECENT NEWS (yield: 2 excerpts · 4 searches logged) ---

Section E contains FOUR excerpt lines, not two: the joint-venture pause, the layoff notice,
the vice-president promotions, and the chief executive's statement. The build record agrees
with the items and not with the header — section 2a of the raw packet reads "E excerpts: 4
(2 events)", and section 2f records both events.

The header looks like a transposition: the excerpt count and the event count have been
swapped, so it should read "yield: 4 excerpts (2 events) · 4 searches logged".

Why it matters beyond tidiness: a rater who reads the header as authoritative sees a section
declaring less evidence than it contains, and section E is exactly the section the proposed
0.7 rule leans on for non-company-authored support. Undercounting it understates the
corroboration available.

The header is reproduced verbatim in the scrubbed packet, wrong count and all.


CD-2  ATTRIBUTION CONTRADICTS THE EXCERPT — section B, rev-04
--------------------------------------------------------------
The 2026-07-22 Support Specialist review carries the source review headline

    "Overall good company to work for"

while the body of the same review is uniformly negative: the first manager left, the second
was "terrible, no prior management experience", the internal union "was useless when it
actually came to needing their help", and "shop stewards were a joke". The truncation cuts
mid-sentence on "President wasn't", so nothing later in the body reconciles the two.

This is the P-06 shape — an attribution title contradicting the reviewer's own body text.
It is possible the headline is genuinely what the reviewer typed (reviewers do write
mismatched headlines, and Indeed prints a separate star rating that the packet does not
carry). But the packet gives the rater no way to tell, and the star rating that would settle
it was not collected.

It is worth more than a single line because this entry is TRIPLICATED: the source site
renders it three times and all three entries are retained under A3, so the contradiction is
put in front of the rater three times over. If the headline is an artefact rather than the
reviewer's own words, the packet is amplifying an artefact.

Suggested build-side follow-up: capture the per-review star rating for section B, or drop
the headline field. One of the two disambiguates this class of defect permanently.


CD-3  STATED COUNT DISAGREES WITH THE NUMBER OF ITEMS — section B header
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
The section B marker reads:

    --- B. EMPLOYEE REVIEWS (15, one per line, most recent first) ---

Seventeen excerpt lines follow. The NOTE TO RATER immediately beneath the marker does
reconcile it — 15 voices, 17 entries, because one review is rendered three times by the
source site — so this is a labelling ambiguity rather than a miscount, and it is recorded
at lower severity than CD-1 for that reason.

It is recorded at all because the stated count and the item count disagree on the face of
the section, and because any tooling that parses the header count and compares it to the
parsed bullet count will flag P-07 as malformed. Section A ("8 excerpts"), section C ("10
excerpts from 4 open postings"), section D ("6 passages across 3 sources") and section F
("6 excerpts") all parse clean; B and E do not.

Suggested wording, decided build-side and not applied here: "(15 reviews in 17 entries, one
per line, most recent first)".
