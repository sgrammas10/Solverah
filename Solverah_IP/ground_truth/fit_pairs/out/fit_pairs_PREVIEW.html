How well would it work for them?

Fit judgements for Solverah. Rater PREVIEW.

Thank you for doing this. It is unpaid, and it is the only way we have to check our model against people who had no hand in building it.

Each screen shows one made-up person and one made-up company. On the left is what the person says they want from a job, on each of six traits, and how far a job can be from that before it bothers them. Up to two traits may be must-haves for them. On the right is what research found about the company on the same six traits, with a short paraphrase of the evidence. Sometimes the evidence disagrees, and sometimes there is too little to say.

Your question each time: if this person took a job at this company, how well do you think it would work for them? Use your own judgement. There are no right answers, and we are not checking you against anything. "Can't say from what's here" is a real answer: use it whenever you mean it.

Judge only from the page. Assume everything else (pay, manager, location, the work itself) is fine and the same at every company. Please don't look anything up, and don't compare notes with other raters until you have finished.
