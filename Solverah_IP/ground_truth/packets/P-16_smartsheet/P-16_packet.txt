S2. B7's TRUNCATION DETECTION ASSUMES A MARKER THAT THE RENDERER DOES NOT PRINT, AND v4.11 PUSHES

    Sections: B7; D27; D28; the retrieval-method section.
    B7 says to mark the cut "where the visible text ends mid-review". v4.11, reasoning from
    P-11, says the renderer returning literal "...Show more" markers "is the only reason the

    "...Show more" on nine of twenty reviews and the renderer printed no marker at all, ending

    truncation by clustering review-body character lengths across two renderer passes (the cut
    falls at 295–310 characters; the two passes agreed exactly) and cross-checked the result
    against plain fetch's marker positions, where they matched one for one. The prompt describes
    neither method. Structural, and consequential: the prompt-compliant path of
    renderer-plus-look-for-a-marker yields 0% truncation on this company, drops the NOTE TO
    RATER that B7 requires above one third, and ships a Section B asserting something false
    about its own sample. P-11's D28 says one pass over a review page is not a measurement; this
    build says one METHOD over a review page is not a measurement either.
