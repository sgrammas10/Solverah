  Header, verbatim:  --- B. EMPLOYEE REVIEWS (12–15, one per line, most recent first) ---
  Actual bullets:    11
  The post-build correction of 2026-08-27 removed one review as a confirmed different company,
  taking the section from 12 to 11, and the header was not updated. Section 2a records the
  shortfall honestly ("B reviews: 11/12") and the SCOPE DECLARATION and NOTE TO RATER (3 of 3)

  the required source is exhausted at eleven. The header's range is unachievable for this
  company, which is itself worth recording: it is the required-yield floor that is wrong here,
  not the collection.

CD-5 — Section D's VOICE COMPOSITION FLAG miscounts and mislabels.
  Verbatim:  "VOICE COMPOSITION FLAG FOR THE RATER: three of the four passages below are
             senior-leadership or executive voice (Deputy Chief Information Security Officer;
             Chief Executive Officer). One is an individual-contributor engineer."
  Two problems, both affecting how a rater weights the section.
  (a) The parenthetical names two sources against a count of three. Section 2a resolves the
      third — the public-repository maintainer collective, "treated as company voice" — but the
      rater-facing flag does not say so, so the flag reads as internally inconsistent.
  (b) "the four passages below" refers to the four distinct SOURCES. The section holds eleven
      excerpts, and 2a says so ("D passages: 11 excerpts across 4 distinct sources"). A rater
      reading "four passages" and counting eleven bullets has no way to reconcile the two, and
      the section header's "yield: 4" repeats the same ambiguity.
  The substance of the flag is right and the scrub left it verbatim; the wording is what needs
  fixing at the build.
