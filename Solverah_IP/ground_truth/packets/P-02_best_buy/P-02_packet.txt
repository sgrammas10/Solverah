########## SECTION 5 - BUILDER'S DEFECT REPORT ON PROMPT v4.6 ##########

STRUCTURAL - changed what evidence reached Section 1, or how a section was scoped.

S1. THE PROMPT MANDATES TWO RETRIEVAL METHODS AND GIVES NO INSTRUCTION FOR HAVING ONLY ONE.
    Related section: the v4.4 two-methods block, and hard rule 4.
    The JavaScript renderer was unavailable for this entire build. The prompt is emphatic
    that plain fetch and a renderer reach different sources and that neither may be treated
    as sufficient - but it says only "if you cannot open pages, stop and say so", which did
    not apply because plain fetch opened pages fine. So the prompt offers a binary (can
    browse / cannot browse) where the real state was one-of-two-methods, and it does not
    say whether that is a stop condition, a proceed-and-record condition, or a defect. I
    made a defensible choice - proceed under the shipping posture and record the limitation
    precisely - but the prompt should say which. Structural because it determined Section
    A's entire source basis: the company's actual careers and values pages were reached and
    returned a "Loading..." shell, and section A was built instead from the corporate site
    and the Code of Ethics. A packet built with a renderer would have a different Section A.

S2. THE PROOF-OF-ABSENCE RULE HAS NO CATEGORY FOR "REACHED, EXISTS, UNREADABLE BY THE ONE
    METHOD I HAVE."
    Related section: hard rules 4 and 5, and the D-ladder proof-of-absence note.
    The prompt gives me "NOT FOUND / NOT ACCESSIBLE" as a single compound outcome and tells
    me to list what I tried. But ladder rung D3 is a case where I can prove the page EXISTS

    RETRIEVABLE, NOT ABSENT and explained, but I invented that label. Step 6 of the build
    brief distinguishes three findings (did not exist / stale / could not be reached); the
    collector prompt's own vocabulary does not. Structural because it changes what a rater
    concludes from a blank rung.

S3. SECTION D'S VOICE-COMPOSITION RULE DOES NOT COVER WHO PUBLISHED THE VOICE.
    Related section: D0, and B2b by analogy.
