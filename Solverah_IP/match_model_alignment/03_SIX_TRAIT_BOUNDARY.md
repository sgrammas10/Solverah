# Boundary statement: the six traits

**Status:** working draft, 2026-10-13. Definitions come from the anchors v3.1 rubric
(which includes your 2026-09-14 rulings) and the model's structure file
(`causal_graph/v1_model/solverah_model_config.yaml`). Anchors v3.1 are still PROPOSED
and not yet verified by raters.

## What the six traits are

Six working-style axes. Each runs between **two legitimate poles**; neither end is
better. On the person side they are the person's **current stated preferences** from the
24-item core instrument. On the company side they describe how work actually happens
there, from evidence.

| Trait | Low pole | High pole | What it captures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Autonomy | Directed | Self-directed | Who decides the how: latitude over approach, priorities and decisions versus assigned direction, process and approval chains. |
| Pace | Steady | Fast | Tempo and intensity: deliberate cadence versus rapid, high-urgency turnaround. |
| Collaboration | Solo | Collective | How work is produced: alone and heads-down versus together, cross-functional, in the room. |
| Feedback | Sparse / gentle | Frequent / blunt | How critique flows: occasional and softened versus frequent and direct. Only critique counts. |
| Innovation | Refine / optimize | Invent / explore | Novelty of the work: improving what exists versus making what does not exist yet. |
| Decision | Intuition / judgement | Data / analysis | What decisions rest on: experience and judgement versus metrics and analysis. |

## What they explicitly do NOT capture

| Not captured | Where it lives instead, if anywhere |
|---|---|
| **Values** (mission, ethics, what the company stands for) | Nowhere yet. |
| **Trust and psychological safety** | Nowhere yet. The rubric excludes it from Feedback on purpose. |
| **Warmth, supportiveness, recognition** | Nowhere. Your ruling: warmth is orthogonal to Feedback and appears at both poles. |
| **Influence and politics** (how power and visibility work) | Nowhere yet. |
| **The manager** | **Manager Match**, a separate instrument and report. Teams and managers are not modelled in the six traits; manager uncertainty becomes interview questions. |
| **The team** | Not modelled. Evidence can be scoped to a role, division or location, but not to a team. |
| **Sustainability** (whether this way of working can last for this person) | An exploratory **outcome**, not a trait. Pace measures tempo, not whether the person can keep it up. |
| **Compensation** | A background adjustment (pay posture relative to the market, 1 to 5), not a trait. Your note that underpay means different things at different career stages is not modelled. |
| **Company size, industry** | Background adjustments, not traits. |
| **Stability** (layoffs, executive churn, financial health) | Nowhere yet. You raised it as a possible seventh signal; still open. |
| **Legitimacy** (self-contradicting or too-good-to-be-true evidence) | Nowhere yet. Your "quarantine" idea is still open. |
| **Growth, learning, enjoyment of the work** | Nowhere yet. Your pace note suggests these can offset a trait gap (see 06). |
| **Lasting needs versus current circumstances** | The Reflection Engine, which is not built. |

## Rules that keep the traits apart

- **Collaboration vs Feedback.** "People raise concerns and managers listen" is
  Collaboration by default. It becomes Feedback only when it carries evaluative,
  corrective, developmental or behavioural input aimed at someone's work (your ruling 2).
- **Autonomy vs Decision.** Following a procedure is Autonomy. A procedure that governs
  how decisions are reached (required analysis, approval layers, escalation, who holds
  the call) is Decision (your ruling 3).
- **Pace vs Innovation.** Speed is not novelty. Optimizing can be frantic; inventing can
  be slow.
- **Autonomy vs Innovation.** How prescribed the method is says nothing about how new the
  output is.

## On deck, not active

Two further axes are defined but switched off: **Structure** (improvisational vs
process-driven) and **Orientation** (task and results vs people and relationships). They
would be added only if the six prove too coarse.
