# Gap combinations that may compound: a draft for you to edit

**Status:** draft, 2026-10-13. **Every item is a hypothesis. The v1 model ignores all of
them in its math:** today each trait's gap is costed on its own and the costs are added.
This list exists so that the pairs you believe matter are written down before any data
arrives, and can be tested later rather than discovered after the fact.

**How to edit.** For each item, mark keep, drop or reword, and add any pair we missed.
Half-finished is fine; whatever you mark is usable.

**Directions used below.** "Job faster than wanted" means the job sits further toward the
high pole than the person prefers. Your elicited answers are quoted as "N in 10 still
doing well", against your baseline of 8 in 10 for a genuine fit.

**Sources.** *Your numbers*: built from your priors-form answers. *Your note*: built
from a free-text note you wrote (priors form, 2026-09-17, or round-1 rater notes). *Ours*:
drafted by us, with no direct basis in what you wrote.
